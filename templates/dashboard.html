Education Section

A unit is 1 kilowatt-hour (kWh). If a 1000W appliance runs for 1 hour, it uses 1 unit.

The system multiplies power, count, and hours to estimate daily usage, then scales it to monthly units automatically.

Use AC at 24°C, charge EVs during off-peak hours, and avoid unnecessary standby loads for steady savings.

UPS and inverter backup usage include a realistic loss overhead so the estimate reflects real household conditions.
