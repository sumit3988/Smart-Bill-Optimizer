Knowledge Base & Help

A unit is 1 kilowatt (kW) of power used for 1 hour.
Example: A 1000W bulb running for 1 hour = 1 unit.
Monthly usage is typically 100-300 units for average households.

Slab System:
• 0-100 units: ₹3/unit
• 101-200 units: ₹5/unit
• 201-500 units: ₹7/unit
• 500+ units: ₹10/unit
Key Insight: Higher slabs cost more, so saving units saves exponentially!

Peak Hours (6-9 PM): Expensive rate
Off-Peak Hours (10 PM - 6 AM): Cheap rate (40% savings)
Tip: Charge EV and run heavy appliances during off-peak hours!
